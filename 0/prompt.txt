Challenge 1: BMW — AI for Product Management

* BMW is attending for the third time with the EHL series
* Challenge focuses on AI-assisted product management for automotive development
* Key problem: product managers must balance brand legacy, market diversity, engineering constraints, customer needs, and competition to define a car's requirements — a single model must sell for 7–8 years
* Participants are asked to build tools that help condense and prioritize feedback and requirements, and document decisions in context
* BMW is actively hiring: internships and a PhD position on AI for deployed engineers; contact people in blue T-shirts

Challenge 2: Atira ([Oxy.LR](http://Oxy.LR)) — AI Agent Fleet for Industrial Sales

* Atira is a Munich startup building AI workforce tools for complex B2B sales processes (fire trucks, trains, airplanes, industrial machines)
* Challenge: build an agent fleet that can navigate a simulated industrial sales environment — including a tender portal, supplier portal, ERP system (SAP-style), and email — and submit a winning bid in under 20 minutes
* A single agent will not work; agents must communicate with each other due to interdependencies (e.g., quantity affects price, product codes affect discounts)
* Scoring: validity of bid → correctness of values → time and LLM spend optimization
* After the deadline, agents run on a private hidden test set to prevent overfitting
* Free unlimited Quentин and Claude tokens provided
* Public leaderboard on GitHub; hard-coded submissions will be flagged
* Atira workshop at 4:00 PM on agent fleet challenges
* Atira is actively hiring

Challenge 3: Taktum (Tacto) — AI Procurement Negotiation Agents

* Tacto is a ~6-year-old Munich startup building procurement software for manufacturing companies
* Challenge: act as a procurement team member at a fictional company ("Hollerau Pumpenwerke") and build agents that negotiate with AI supplier personas to maximize savings across 50 negotiations
* Agents must: prepare for negotiations, conduct multi-turn dialogues, and decide when to close or walk away
* Scoring considers price achieved vs. hidden supplier floor, but also relationship quality — squeezing too hard is penalized
* Today: 20 public test cases; tomorrow: 50 hidden cases
* Live tournament tomorrow at 12:00: teams' agents run simultaneously on a big screen with a live leaderboard; top 10 advance to final pitches
* Large GitHub repo with hundreds of thousands of data points provided
